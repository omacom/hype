# How we work

<Steps style="chevron">
- :search: **Audit** Understand every machine, app and workflow.
- :git-merge: **Align** Agree on defaults, shortcuts and themes.
- :rocket: **Activate** Roll out, measure and improve every day.
- :refresh-cw: **Evolve** Quarterly releases keep the momentum.
</Steps>

# A history of shared growth

<Timeline style="number">
- **2019** Standardize the hardware
  One laptop model for the whole studio.
- **2021** Scale up
  Twice the team, same IT staff.
- **2023** Go keyboard-first
  Shortcuts for everything people do daily.
- **2025** Reset the setup
  An Omarchy pilot on twelve desks.
- **2026** Roll out
  Every desk on one setup.
</Timeline>
